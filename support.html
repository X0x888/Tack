Tack Support

Help for Tack on macOS 15 or later

Need a hand? Email hi@cryptoanalyzer.app. Include your Tack version, macOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. We aim to respond within two business days.

Quick help

Tack is still running after I close its window

Tack stays available from its menu-bar icon so quick capture continues to work. Use Tack → Quit Tack when you want to stop the app completely.

Edge Auto-Hide is not appearing

Enable Edge Auto-Hide in Settings, move the Tack window against a screen edge, then move the pointer away. Hover over the thin edge strip to expand it again.

iCloud notes are not syncing

Confirm that iCloud Drive is available for the same Apple Account on each Mac, then open Settings → iCloud in Tack. Sync is optional and off by default.

The local helper will not connect

Open Settings → Connections, confirm that the helper is enabled, and follow any prompt to approve Tack under System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions. The helper accepts connections only from apps on the same Mac.

How do I back up my notes?

Use Tack's export or full-backup command and save the result to a location you control. Do this before deleting the app or resetting its data.

Reporting a problem

Settings → About can create a diagnostic bundle for you to inspect and attach manually. It is never uploaded automatically. Remove anything you do not want to share before emailing it.

Accessibility

See the Tack accessibility page, or email us if an accessibility issue blocks your workflow.
